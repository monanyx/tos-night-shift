Loop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

What we collect

We collect precise location data, including in the background when the app is closed, to improve route suggestions and measure everyday activity.

We also collect the profile details you give us, like your name, age and fitness goals.

How we use it

We use your workout data to show your stats, build your training plan and keep the app working.

Who we share it with

We share data with service providers who help us run Loop, such as cloud hosting. They may only use it to provide services to us.

We may also share activity and health insights with insurance and wellness partners who offer rewards programs.

How long we keep it

If you delete your account, we may retain de-identified workout history for up to five years.
