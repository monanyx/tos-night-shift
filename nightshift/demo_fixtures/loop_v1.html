Loop Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 12, 2026

What we collect

When you record a run or ride, we collect your route and pace while the workout is active. You can turn off location at any time in Settings.

We also collect the profile details you give us, like your name, age and fitness goals.

How we use it

We use your workout data to show your stats, build your training plan and keep the app working.

Who we share it with

We share data with service providers who help us run Loop, such as cloud hosting. They may only use it to provide services to us.

How long we keep it

If you delete your account, we delete your workout history within 30 days.
