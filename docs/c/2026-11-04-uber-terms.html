### 10. Agents
These terms ("Agent Terms") apply if you, directly or indirectly, use, authorize, or deploy an Agent to access or interact with the Services. An "Agent" is any software, tool, or service that takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action on behalf of, or at the direction of, any person or entity. These Agent Terms are not intended to restrict accessibility tools, such as screen readers, that help individuals with disabilities use the Services.
**Agent Requirements**.
Agents must operate openly and honestly. An Agent must identify itself as an Agent and operate in accordance with the requirements of these Agent Terms. Specifically, in all HTTP/HTTPS requests, an Agent must identify that the request is from an Agent, and Agents must not solve CAPTCHAs. If Uber asks an Agent to stop accessing the Services, the Agent must stop immediately and may not resume without Uber's permission.
**Access and Enforcement**.
Uber may offer programs for approved Agents ("Approved Agent Programs"). Each program has its own terms, which control over these Agent Terms if they conflict. Uber has no obligation to approve any Agent. Except as provided in an Approved Agent Program, Uber may, in its sole discretion and without notice, restrict, condition, or block any Agent's access to the Services, including through technical measures. If you or an Agent acting for you violates these Agent Terms, Uber may also, without notice, suspend or terminate your Account and pursue any other available remedies, including injunctive relief against the Agent's provider.
**Your Responsibility for Agents**.
Agents can make mistakes. You are responsible for any order, booking, payment, or other action an Agent takes on your behalf, including the accuracy of details such as addresses, items, payment methods, and timing. Uber's standard cancellation, refund, and dispute policies apply. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Uber is not responsible for any loss or damage caused by an Agent's errors, omissions, or failures, such as incorrect orders, missed bookings, or erroneous payments. Any dispute about an Agent's performance is between you and the Agent's provider, and you release Uber from related claims.
