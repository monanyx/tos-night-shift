If Uber and its affiliates cannot verify your authorization to use such EBT card, or otherwise determines that your use of such EBT card is invalid, fraudulent, or otherwise unauthorized, Uber and its affiliates may refuse to process your EBT card and may remove the same from your Account.
Your ability to use EBT with certain merchants may depend on the eligibility of such merchants to receive EBT for SNAP Eligible Items. Uber makes no guarantee as to the availability of SNAP Eligible Items or a merchant's eligibility to receive EBT for SNAP Eligible Items.
